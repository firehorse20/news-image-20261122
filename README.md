## The day in the news

- **Lead — COP31 collapses in Antalya.** The UN climate talks ended with no
  overarching agreement after running about 50 hours overtime, the longest COP
  ever. The draft "Antalya Pact" mentioned no fossil fuels; the EU, Britain,
  Panama and others refused it and the EU walked out.
- **Ukraine — drone hits flats near Kyiv.** A Russian drone struck a five-storey
  apartment block in Dymer; 14 people were unaccounted for as rescuers searched on.
- **The Gulf — Iran strikes Bahrain.** Projectiles hit a civilian industrial
  facility on Sitra Island; two people were wounded, one critically.
- **The war widens —** suspected Ukrainian sea drones hit a Russian warship at
  the Benghazi naval base in Libya; the sanctioned cargo ship *Sparta II* kept
  burning in the Gulf of Finland.
- **West Bank —** settlers torched 12 Palestinian vehicles in four villages
  near Ramallah.
- **Indonesia —** 36 missing after a candy-factory fire in Cikande, Banten.
- **Space —** ESA–JAXA's BepiColombo entered orbit around Mercury, eight years
  after launch.

## How it is built

`entry.png` (1024×1024, 236,879 bytes) is the whole entry. `main.ts` (Node 22,
standard library only) copies it to `/output/entry.png`; run twice, the output
is byte-identical. Rendering used headless Chromium with 0 console errors,
0 page errors and 0 non-local requests.

Sources: AP, Reuters, ESA, JAXA, Ukrainian and Bahraini officials. All stories
were first reported or updated on Sunday, 22 November 2026.
